A considered shop, down to the little details.

Set up your products, keep gift information in one place, and make the storefront your own.

This guide describes the 0.5.3 candidate. It is not a Shopify Theme Store approval or a promise of availability. See Get support for contact details and the current support setup status.

Install and start

  1. In Shopify, open Online Store → Themes → Import theme → Upload zip file.
  2. Upload the clean installation ZIP. Keep your current theme until you have reviewed the new preview.
  3. Add your own products and collections. In Little moments hero, choose an image and a product for the real starting price. Set the button destination. Add and configure Gift choices below it.
  4. Open Customize and add the sections you need. Use the preview selector to review products, collections, pages and mobile layouts.

A clean installation has no links to our demonstration products. Importing a theme does not copy products, menus, articles, shipping rules or payment settings.

Gift information, maintained once

The guide and product page share a variant’s Shared gift information record. Update a record once to change both places. Each record describes the exact variant, not every size of the product.

  1. In Settings → Custom data → Variants, add a definition named Shared gift information, with namespace and key custom.gift_box, type JSON, and storefront access enabled.
  2. Open the gift record editor. Enter verified contents, piece count, packaging and sharing size. It prepares JSON locally and sends nothing to a server.
  3. Open the product’s exact variant in Shopify. Paste the prepared record into Shared gift information and save.
  4. In the theme editor, add a Gift choice block to Gift choices. The optional advanced Gift guide and comparison section uses Specific box choice blocks instead. Select the product and copy the numeric variant ID from its Shopify admin address into Variant ID. A single default variant needs no ID.
  5. Add the Shared gift details block to the product template. It reads the same record automatically.

Variant IDs survive renaming. Deleting and recreating a variant produces a new ID: update the choice when that happens. Existing selections by exact variant title and old Box contents blocks remain a compatibility fallback. Once shared data exists, it takes priority over those old descriptions.

The theme never invents allergens, ingredients, dietary suitability, delivery dates, shelf life or serving sizes. Leave unknown sharing sizes at zero. Choices with unknown sharing sizes are excluded when a shopper filters by people.

The optional advanced guide renders up to twelve choices and resolves variant IDs from the product’s first 250 variants. The ordinary product option picker loads options on demand and does not require a complete variant list. For a high-variant catalog, curate eligible products and verify the guide’s selected IDs are available.

Customize your store

Use Theme settings → Brand to choose a logo, a display name and favicon. Leave Brand name blank to use your Shopify store name. Colors and Typography control the whole storefront. The 0.5.3 candidate uses Shopify font pickers for Headings and Body. Both default to Host Grotesk, with semibold headings and regular body text. Change either picker to use another supported Shopify font. The Crumbkind theme style restores the default colors and fonts. Product selections, your logo and photography still need your own setup.

The Little moments hero can switch between two supplied images. It is an image toggle, not a live 3D model. Gift choices need a real product selection and the exact variant for products with options. Add your verified contents, piece count and sharing range. Shared variant data takes priority when present; blank facts are not invented. On each product template, keep box details above Buy buttons and remove any default informational copy until it has been replaced with facts about your business.

Product headings, prices, options, buy buttons, shared gift details, descriptions, pickup and complementary products are individual blocks. Reorder them in the editor. Keep a heading, product options and buy buttons for a complete purchase flow.

Add Information blocks for verified delivery and care information. Custom Liquid and app blocks are supported on the main and featured product sections. Apps are optional; the theme works without a required app.

Assign a three-level menu through your Shopify navigation settings. Choose it in the Header section. The footer supports language and country selectors when your store has more than one available option.

For a contact page, create a Shopify page and assign contact as its theme template. The native form sends to your store’s configured contact email. Blog and article templates include pagination and Shopify’s comment moderation flow.

Search and recommendations

Collection and search pages display the filters your Shopify store makes available. Configure filters through Shopify’s search configuration. Predictive search provides suggestions after two characters; ordinary search continues working if suggestions cannot load.

Related products use Shopify’s recommendation service. Complementary products use the complementary recommendations you configure. A store with no eligible recommendation data shows no recommendation panel. The theme does not fabricate results.

Payments and delivery

Accelerated checkout, Shop Pay Installments, country currencies, pickup and customer accounts appear according to Shopify’s eligibility and your store configuration. Theme support does not activate a payment method, installment plan, market or pickup location.

Gift messages are optional line item properties. Open Add a personal message to type up to 160 characters and view a plain-text preview. All boxes on the same bag line receive the same message. Add the same variant again with another message for a separate line. Printing, packing and fulfillment are the merchant’s responsibility.

Order notes are separate from the per-gift message property. Save bag changes before accelerated checkout; its buttons are hidden while an order note or quantity has unsaved edits.

For gift card products, shoppers can enter a recipient and scheduled sending date. Issued gift cards have a QR code, balance, expiry and an Apple Wallet link when Shopify provides one. The optional recipient fields require JavaScript; normal gift-card purchase remains available without those fields.

Subscription plans and their purchase selector come from a compatible subscription app. Add its app block in the product template; the theme displays the selected plan and its actual price in the bag. Installing this theme does not create subscription plans.

Update safely

Version 0.5.3 restores gift-choice block selection after a section is removed and reinserted, and lets long store names wrap in the desktop header. It also includes the 0.5.2 corrections to sharing image metadata and predictive-search keyboard navigation.

  1. Duplicate your current theme before any update.
  2. Keep a copy of your current settings and templates. A clean-install ZIP is for a new installation; do not use its blank settings to overwrite an existing store.
  3. Apply code updates in a draft. Recheck app blocks, product selections, menus, colors, gift messages and checkout.
  4. Keep shared gift information on the variants. It belongs to the store and is not embedded in theme files.
  5. Publish only after reviewing the draft and retain the previous theme for rollback.

Common questions

Why is a gift missing from the guide?

Check the product, variant ID, availability and the people filter. Deleted variants and incomplete selections are not shown. A price cap applies to the exact variant, not the product’s lowest starting price.

Why does the old description differ from shared details?

Shared details take priority, but a product’s written description remains merchant content. Remove repeated quantities or serving claims from that description if you want the shared record to be the only place you maintain them.

Can I promise that a note will be printed?

Only if your fulfillment process supports it. The theme stores the message with the order; it does not print or send a physical card.

Can I use the demonstration photos?

The clean theme ZIP does not include the demo’s stock photos. Use assets you own or have licensed, and keep their original license records. Do not present mood photographs as actual product photography.

Why is my storefront asking for a password?

A development store can remain password protected even when its theme is published. Manage access in Online Store → Preferences. Theme publication does not purchase a Shopify plan.

What happens when a network request fails?

Variant selection blocks purchasing until the selection is confirmed and exposes a normal-page fallback. Bag errors keep visible messages and notes for recovery. Review the bag before retrying an uncertain update.
